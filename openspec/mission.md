# Mission

A personal learning platform where **knowledge lives as interconnected nodes**, **AI agents accelerate you without taking the wheel**, and **the platform is yours to extend, port, and remix**.

## What it is

A downloadable, open-source learning environment for macOS where you:

- **Build** knowledge as a graph of nodes — concepts, sources, conversations, practice sessions
- **Converse** with AI agents grounded in your own material — the agent sees your nodes, your files, your past sessions
- **Practice** what you've learned — code, Q&A, written response, multiple choice
- **Branch** any conversation into a new node that inherits the context you choose
- **Study on demand** — describe a topic and a way you want to learn it; the platform assembles nodes, files, agents, and practice around it

## Core Principles

1. **Nodes First** — Every piece of knowledge is a node. No orphan notes.
2. **Links as Learning** — Relationships between nodes are as important as the nodes themselves.
3. **Exploration Over Search** — Navigate by following connections, not typing queries.
4. **Collaborative AI** — Agents accelerate your thinking; you stay in the driver's seat. The agent sees what you choose to share and forgets what you choose to compact.
5. **Hierarchical Memory** — Long conversations compress into summaries; summaries can be drilled back into. You decide what persists and at what fidelity.
6. **Minimal Friction** — Creating a node, asking the agent, or starting practice should be as fast as typing a thought.
7. **Yours Forever** — Local-first, plain-text where possible, BYOK for AI access, no vendor lock-in.
8. **Open Ecosystem** — macOS first, but the architecture invites community ports (Linux, Windows, CLI, web). Skills, MCP servers, and provider adapters are pluggable.

## Anti-Goals

- Not a replacement for tutors — an amplifier.
- Not a closed garden — every component has an escape hatch (export, plain Markdown, raw SQLite).
- Not a billing platform — you bring your own AI keys.
